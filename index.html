Synopsis

For millennia the highest thinkers pursued the impossible dream of shaping reality through pure intent. Ancient alchemists spoke of transmuting base matter through sacred incantations and geometries. In our age that dream has migrated: the mystical ether is now the digital medium, and the programmer, freed from low-level ritual by Vibe Coding, returns to the status of alchemical architect.

Between the Type — the builder of ordered systems — and the Antitype — the breaker of every boundary — beneath the gaze of the Apex Mind, the living Garden of Code grows.
